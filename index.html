What's inside

Everything you need to support your Korean learning

All features work fully offline. No internet, no account, no ads.

Offline Dictionary

Search over 61,000 entries in English, Korean, or Japanese with definitions in each. Rich entry details that include conjugation tables, hanja breakdown, related words, example sentences, high quality text-to-speech, and more.

Hanja Explorer for CJK learners

Search and explore Hanja characters with readings across Korean, Japanese, Mandarin, and Cantonese. See every word that uses a given character.

Conjugation Tables

Instant conjugation tables for any verb or adjective across all speech levels and tenses. Tap any form in the table to hear it pronounced.

SRS Flashcards

Maximize retention while minimizing how many reviews you need to do. Create custom word and Hanja decks then review them with built-in spaced repetition flashcards.

Learning Hub

Browse common words by category — Family, Food, Daily Life, and more. Learn Hangul with 10 guided lessons. Searchable TOPIK grammar reference.

No Account. No Ads.

All data stored locally on your device. Romanization support, dark and light mode, optional Japanese search, and Pinyin/Jyutping for compound Hanja words.
